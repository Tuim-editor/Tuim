# In-App Bug Reporting

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **WORK IN PROGRESS**
> The in-app bug reporting subsystem is currently being redesigned and will be updated in an upcoming release.

---

## Overview

Tuim aims to provide a seamless, privacy-conscious bug reporting experience directly from the terminal. 

Currently, to report bugs or unexpected behavior:
* Please open an issue on GitHub at: [https://github.com/Rouboufy/tuim/issues](https://github.com/Rouboufy/tuim/issues)
* Include your operating system, terminal emulator, Tuim version (`tuim --version`), and steps to reproduce.

Detailed in-app reporting documentation will be published here once the redesign is complete.
